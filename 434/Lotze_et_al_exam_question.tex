\question[20]
\begin{minipage}[t][2in]{0.32\linewidth}%
In their paper, \emph{Recovery of marine animal populations and
ecosystems}, Lotze et al. discuss several drivers of recovery. Pick any
any three drivers (your choice) that must be considered / implemented to
shift a degraded reef that is an algal-dominated stable state to a
healthy coral-dominated stable state (figure at right). Explain how each
driver might cause the desired outcome.\vfill
\end{minipage}\hfil\begin{minipage}[t][2in]{0.6\textwidth}
\vspace{-1ex}\includegraphics[width=\textwidth]{exam3_fig2}
\end{minipage}%

\vspace{3cm}

\begin{solution}

Drivers:

\begin{itemize}

	\item Exploitation.
	\item Habitat loss.
	\item Pollution.
	\item Favorable life history conditions.
	\item Favorable environmental conditions.
	\item Awareness
	\item Legal protection
	\item Enforcement of management plants.

\end{itemize}

2 points per driver, plus 4 points for each explanation.

Nearly any explanation for a driver is good as long as it is realistic and well reasoned.

\end{solution}
