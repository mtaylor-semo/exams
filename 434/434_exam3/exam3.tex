\question[5]
Explain the cause of the zonation gradient observed on hydrothermal vents.
 
\AnswerBox{1\baselineskip}{0.5}{%
	The gradient is determined by water temp. Species best able to tolerate
	the hot water will be closest to the vent. Those with low thermal
	tolerance will be farthest from the vent.
}
