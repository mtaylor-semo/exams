\question[10]
Describe the conditions that favor cockholdry in \textit{Lepomis} sunfishes. Name and describe both types of strategies used by young males.

\begin{solution}
Large males establish territories for nesting. Smaller males unable to compete for territories would not be able to mate. \textit{Sneaker} males are the smallest males. They sneak in when female releases eggs, releasing sperm, and then dashing out of nest. \textit{Satellite} males mimic females, which allows them to approach the male in the nest. They also release sperm when actual female releases eggs.
\end{solution}
