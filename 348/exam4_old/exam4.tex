\question[5]
Use salinity and light to explain two distinct reasons why coral reefs are not found near estuaries.
	\begin{solution}
	Dilute salinity from freshwater and turbidity from sediments/nutrient enrichment.
	\end{solution}
\vspace*{\stretch{1}}

\question[5]
Describe one specific example of how a deep-sea organism uses bioluminescence.
	\begin{solution}
	Alarm, attract prey, counterillumination, attract mates. 
	\end{solution}
\vspace*{\stretch{1}}

\question[5]
The deepest regions of the bathypelagic zone have less than 1\% of the biomass that is found in zones directly above it.  Explain one reason why.
	\begin{solution}
	Most primary production comes from the surface in various forms. Most primary production is consumed in the epiplagic or mesopelagic so little makes it to the bathypelagic. Less primary production means less biomass can be supported.
	\end{solution}
\vspace*{\stretch{1}}

State whether the whipnose seadevil more likely lives in the mesopelagic or bathypelagic zone, and explain one adaptation, other than bioluminescence and \emph{visible in the image}, the fish exhibits to justify your answer. \question
	\begin{parts}
	\part[5]
	Illustrate a profile of a barrier reef from shoreline to open ocean. Label each zone.
		\begin{solution}
		Fore-reef slope, reef crest, reef flat, back-reef slope, lagoon. Accept patch-reef.
		\end{solution}
	\vspace*{\stretch{0.75}}

	\part[10]
	For each zone you just identified, describe a representative scleractinean coral shape or structure you will find in that zone. Relate the shape or structure of the coral to one primary physical process (e.g., light, temperature, tidal movement, turbidity, wave action, or others, as appropriate) present in each zone. 
		\begin{solution}
		Fore-reef slope has massive or large plate corals adapted for different light regimes / capture light.\\
		Reef crest tends to have heavy branched corals to break up wave action. Also light tolerant, resisist some arial exposure.\\
		Reef flat may have more delicate corals but able to withstand high light intensity / arial exposure.\\
		Back reef slope tends to be stouter branched corals because more protected.\\
		Lagoon has patch reefs.
		\end{solution}
	\vspace*{\stretch{1}}
	\end{parts}
