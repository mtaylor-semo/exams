\question[4]
With regard to energy and carbon sources, describe the one difference and the one similarity between a photoautotroph and a chemoautotroph. 

	\begin{solution}
	Photoautotroph gets energy from light;\\
	Chemoautotroph gets energy from inorganic compounds. (2 points)\\
	Both get carbon from inorganic compounds (2 points)
	\end{solution}

\question[10]
On screen is a picture of a juvenile blueface angelfish. Describe at least two different ways in which the coloration and pattern help to protect this fish from predation on the coral reef.
	\begin{solution}
	The pattern creates disruptive coloration that breaks up the overall outline of the body. (4 points)\\
	The blue and yellow matches the background of the water and reef, respectively, which acts as a type of camouflage. (6 points)
	\end{solution}

\question[10]
Many nudibranchs are brightly colored and therefore highly visible to potential predators.  The colors are hypothesized to serve as warning coloration.  To support this hypothesis, briefly describe one defense mechanism that nudibranchs use to guard against predation.

\begin{solution}
Nudibranchs feed on cnidarians.  The nudibranchs are able to incorporate the cnidocytes from the cnidarians into their own skin.  Any predator that attacks the nudibranch will be stung by the cnidocytes.

2 points for saying they feed on cnidarians. 
2 points for saying that the cnidocytes are incorporated into the skin.
2 points for saying that the predators will be stung.
2 points for overall quality of explanation.
\end{solution}
\vspace*{\stretch{1}}
