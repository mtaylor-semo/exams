\question[5]
A brittle sea star lives on the abyssal plain in the middle of the Indian Ocean.  What is the salinity of this organism's environment? What is the temperature ($^\circ$C)?
	\begin{solution}
	Salinity = 35 ppt (2 points). Temp = 4$^\circ$C (2 points). 5 pt deduction if entirely wrong.
	\end{solution}
