\question[5]
Briefly explain bioturbation and how it affects the depth of the aerobic zone in a soft-bottom littoral community.
	\begin{solution}
	Bioturbation occurs when burrowing organisms disturb the sediment. This increases oxygen penetration deeper into the sediments, deeping the aerobic zone. 
	\end{solution}
