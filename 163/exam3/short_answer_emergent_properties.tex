\question[5]
What is meant by ``emergent properties'' when discussing the hierarchical organization of life? Give an example.

\AnswerBox{5\baselineskip}{%\begin{solution}
New properties emerge that are not present a lower hierarchical levels. (3 pts) For example, populations do not have predator-prey interactions but communities do. Any example will suffice as along as reasonable. (2 pts)
}%\end{solution}
