\begin{multicols}{2}
You performed a \textit{t}-test to compare a variable between two populations.
The sample size from \emph{each} population was 4 individuals. You calculated
$t = 2.39.$ Using $\alpha = 0.05$, do you accept or reject the null hypothesis?
Explain how you used the table at right to decide, including specific numbers.

\begin{solution}

Accept null hypothesis. Degrees of freedom is 10, so critical $t = 2.23.$ 
Calculated $t = 2.21,$ which is lower than the table value. If the calculated
value is lower than the tabled value, then you accept (or fail to reject) the null hypothesis.

\end{solution}
