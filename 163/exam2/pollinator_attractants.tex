\question[4]
A bumble bee visits a bright yellow sunflower to collect pollen for its hive. As the bee visits different flowers, the flowers get pollinated. Identify the \textit{specific} primary and secondary attractants from this example. Tell how you know.

\AnswerBox{0.1\textheight}{%
	The color is a secondary attractant. It serves to attract the bee but does not provide a reward. The pollen is the primary attractant. It provides the reward that the bee can take back to the hive.
}
