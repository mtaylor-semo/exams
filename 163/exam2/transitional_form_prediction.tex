\fullwidth{\noindent The following hypothesis makes specific \emph{predictions} about
transitional forms between organisms and about when the forms should
appear in the fossil record. (\textsc{mya} = millions of years ago) }
%\emph{Explain your reasoning for questions~\ref{ques:fossil_predictions} and \ref{ques:fossil_evidence}}.
\begin{center}
	\includegraphics{exam2_transitional_forms}
\end{center}

\question[10]\label{ques:fossil_predictions}
Identify \emph{all} transitional forms that are \emph{predicted} by this hypothesis.
Based on this hypothesis, when is each transitional form
\emph{predicted} to appear in the fossil record?

\begin{AnswerPage}{0.3\textheight}

2 points each transition.\vspace{\baselineskip}

Jawed fishes to amphibian/mammal ca. 150 \textsc{mya}. (amphib \emph{or} mammal \textsc{ok}.)\\
Jawed fishes to bird/fish/reptile/ ca. 150 \textsc{mya}. (Likewise)\\
Amphib to mammal ca. 60 \textsc{mya}.\\
fish (or other appropriate name) to bird ca. 90 \textsc{mya}.\\
fish to reptile ca. 30 \textsc{mya}.\\

\end{AnswerPage}

\question[10]\label{ques:fossil_evidence}
You find evidence in the fossil record that allows you to test the
above hypothesis. You find the following transitional forms in the
fossil record at the following geological ages: \vspace{\baselineskip}

fish/amphibian: 375 million years ago

reptile/mammal: 290 million years ago

first jawed fishes: 490 million years ago

reptile/bird: 200 million years ago

amphibian/reptile: 325 million years ago\vspace{\baselineskip}

Thoroughly explain \emph{all} of the ways in which your new evidence
supports or falsifies the hypothesis shown on the previous page.

\begin{AnswerPage}{0.6\textheight}

2 points each. Wording depends on what organisms they used in their predictions. Judge accordingly, watching for consistency. Possible valid statements include \vspace{\baselineskip}

Fish/amphib transition supported by time wrong (150 vs 375 textsc{mya}).\\
Reptile/mammal not predicted so falsified. Time not really relevant.\\
first jawed fishes appeared 490 \textsc{mya}, not 180 \textsc{mya}.\\
reptile/bird predicted by time wrong (90 vs 200 \textsc{mya}).\\
amphib/reptile not predicted. Time not relevant.\\
fish/reptile was predicted but not found. \\


\end{AnswerPage}
