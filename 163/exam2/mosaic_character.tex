\question[4]\label{ques:fossil_predictions}
Clearly explain what is meant by “mosaic evolution” or the “mosaic character of evolution” and how it relates to transitional forms found in the fossil record.  Support your explanation with two specific traits observed in the reptile / bird fossils shown in class. 


\AnswerBox{0.2\textheight}{%
Characters evolve at different rates so transitional forms have some ancestral and some descendant characters.
}
