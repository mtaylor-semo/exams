\question[12]
The table shows the presence and absence of seven homologies (1–7) among eight species (A–H). Use these data to draw a phylogenetic tree. Draw a tick mark across the branch of each common ancestor and label it with the homologous character (from the table) that unites each clade.

%\vspace*{\baselineskip}

\begin{longtable}[l]{@{}lrrrrrrr@{}}
\toprule
	& 1	& 2	& 3	& 4	& 5	& 6	& 7	\tabularnewline
\midrule
A	& 1	& 0	& 0	& 1	& 1	& 1 & 0	\tabularnewline
B	& 0	& 0	& 0	& 0	& 0	& 1 & 1	\tabularnewline
C	& 1	& 1	& 0	& 0	& 0	& 1 & 0	\tabularnewline
D	& 1	& 0	& 1	& 1	& 0	& 1 & 0	\tabularnewline
E	& 1	& 1	& 0	& 0	& 0	& 1 & 0	\tabularnewline
F	& 1	& 0	& 1	& 1	& 0	& 1 & 0	\tabularnewline
G	& 1	& 0	& 0	& 1	& 1	& 1 & 0 \tabularnewline
H	& 0	& 0	& 0	& 0	& 0	& 1	& 1	\tabularnewline
\bottomrule
\end{longtable}

\vspace*{2\baselineskip}
\begin{AnswerPage}{0.4\textheight}

\centering

\begin{forest}
  mytree,
    [[,name=ALL
		[,name=AGDFC
			[,name=AGDF
				[,name=AG
					[A]
					[G]
				]
				[,name=DF
					[D]
					[F]
				]
			]
			[,name=CE
				[C]
				[E]
			]
		]
		[,name=BH
		 [B]
		 [H]
		]
    ]]
    \draw [thick] ([xshift=-2mm, yshift=-5mm] AG.base)--+(0:4mm) node [right] {5};
    \draw [thick] ([xshift=-2mm, yshift=-5mm] DF.base)--+(0:4mm) node [right] {3};
    \draw [thick] ([xshift=-2mm, yshift=-5mm] AGDF.base)--+(0:4mm) node [right] {4};
    \draw [thick] ([xshift=-2mm, yshift=-5mm] CE.base)--+(0:4mm) node [right] {2};
    \draw [thick] ([xshift=-2mm, yshift=-5mm] AGDFC.base)--+(0:4mm) node [right] {1};
    \draw [thick] ([xshift=-2mm, yshift=-5mm] ALL.base)--+(0:4mm) node [right] {6};
    \draw [thick] ([xshift=-2mm, yshift=-5mm] BH.base)--+(0:4mm) node [right] {7};
\end{forest}

\end{AnswerPage}
