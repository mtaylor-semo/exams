\question[4]\label{first_phylo}
Which are most closely related, cleaning shrimp and night shrimp, or key hole urchin and rock-boring urchin?

	\begin{solution}
		Urchins. They share the same common ancestor. 
		The shrimp trace back one additional common ancestor.
	\end{solution}
\vspace*{\stretch{1}}

\question[4]
Which organism(s), if any, is (are) most closely related to a jewel urchin?
	\begin{solution}
		Sea egg and pea urchin are most closely related. They share the most recent common ancestor with jewel urchin.
	\end{solution}

\question[3]\label{last_phylo}
Suppose you find a homology between slipper lobster and spider crab. Is the
hypothesis supported, falsified, or is the evidence inconclusive?
	\begin{solution}
		The hypothesis predicts the slipper lobster and spider crab have a common ancestor and therefore should have a homology. If a homology is found, the hypothesis is supported.
	\end{solution}
