\question[3]
The Impatiens population (previous page) contains an excess of both homozygotes but fewer heterozygotes than expected based on actual allele frequencies.  Which mode of selection would fit the observed pattern?  Explain why. 

\AnswerBox{0.25\textheight}{
Disruptive selection (1 points). Have more of both homozygotes but fewer heterozygotes, suggesting both extreme phenotypes are being selected for / heterozygotes are being selected against. (2 points).
}
