\question[16]
List below the four missing steps of the Scientific Method. Then,
for each step, write a statement that illustrates how scientists applied
the scientific method to the experiments described below. I have
provided step four as an example. 

Biologists wondered how homing pigeons are able return to their home
roost from any direction and distance. One idea that had been proposed 
was that the pigeons orient using the Earth's magnetic fields, much like 
a compass. To test this idea, scientists built a flight chamber that was 
surrounded by a magnetic field. The scientists could adjust the field to 
change the apparent location of the magnetic north pole. After homing 
pigeons were placed in the chamber, the scientists
changed the location of the apparent North Pole to several different
locations around the chamber. In all cases, the pigeons changed their
 orientation to match the adjusted magnetic North Pole. Their
results provided strong evidence that homing pigeons use the earth's
magnetic fields for orientation.
