\question[4]
The Impatiens population (previous page) contains an excess of one homozygote but fewer of the other two genotypes than expected based on actual allele frequencies.  Which mode of selection would fit the observed pattern?  Explain why. 

\AnswerBox{0.2\textheight}{
Directional selection (1 points). Have more of one homozygote fewer of the heterozygotes and the other homozygote, suggesting one extreme phenotypes is being selected for. (2 points).
}
