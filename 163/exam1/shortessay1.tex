\question[6]
A scientist wants to test the hypothesis that a population of a flowering plant that lives high on a mountain side will bloom later than a population of the same plant that lives near the base of the mountain. Use this information to identify the explanatory and response variables. Tell how you know.

\AnswerBox{4\baselineskip}{%
Explanatory variable is the altitude because that is controlled by the scientist. The response variable is the time of blooming because it responds to the altitude.
}
