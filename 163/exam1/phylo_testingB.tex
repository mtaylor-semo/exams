\question[4]\label{first_phylo}
Which are most closely related, black widow and brown recluse, or tree hopper and leaf hopper?

	\begin{solution}
		The two hoppers because they share the same common ancestor. 
		Widow and recluse trace back one additional common ancestor.
	\end{solution}
\vspace*{\stretch{1}}

\question[4]
Which organism(s), if any, is (are) most closely related to a robber fly?
	\begin{solution}
		fruit fly and dung fly are most closely related. They share the most recent common ancestor with robber fly.
	\end{solution}

\question[3]\label{last_phylo}
Suppose you find a homology between peacock spider and deer tick. Is
the hypothesis supported, falsified, or is the evidence inconclusive?
	\begin{solution}
		Falsified. The hypothesis predicts that peacock spider and deer tick do not have a common ancestor. Therefore, they should not have a homology. If a homology is found, then the hypothesis is falsified.
	\end{solution}
