\question[3]
The Impatiens population (previous page) contains an excess of heterozygotes but fewer of both homozygotes than expected.  Which mode of selection would fit this pattern?  Explain why. 

\AnswerBox{0.25\textheight}{
Stabilizing selection (1 points). Have more heterozygotes and fewer of both homozygotes, suggesting both extreme phenotypes are being selected against / heterozygotes are being selected for. (2 points).
}
