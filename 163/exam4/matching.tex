\question\MatchQuestion{Net primary production}{Amount of sun's energy transferred to primary consumers as organic matter.}
\question\MatchQuestion{Primary producer}{Responsible for gross primary production.}
\question\MatchQuestion{Eutrophication}{Excess nutrients in an ecosystem.}
\question\MatchQuestion{Mean Ann. Precip.}{Determines climate in an area in combination with mean annual temperature.}
\question\MatchQuestion{Trophic cascade}{Upper trophic level species causes alternating abundance levels of lower trophic level species.}
\question\MatchQuestion{Biomass}{The mass of living organisms in an area.}
\question\MatchQuestion{Decomposer}{Group of organisms that get energy from dead organisms.}
\question\MatchQuestion{Early succession}{Composed mostly of grasses and other generalist species.}
\question\MatchQuestion{Mutualism}{Both species get ecological benefit over the long term.}
\question\MatchQuestion{Tolerance}{A pioneer species neither helps or harms other species during succession.}
