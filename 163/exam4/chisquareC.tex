A scientist gave 35 individuals a choice of five habitats. She predicted that more
individuals would be found in chamber 5 compared to chambers 1–4. Her results are shown in the figure. Her
calculated $\chi^2$ was 9.43.

\begin{minipage}[t]{0.65\textwidth}

\medskip

\includegraphics[width=\textwidth]{exam4_chisquareC}

\bigskip

\question[2]
How many degrees of freedom did she have? \ifprintanswers \textbf{{\tablenumbers 4}} \else \AnswerBlank \fi 

\bigskip

\question[2]
What critical value of $\chi^2$ did she use? \ifprintanswers \textbf{{\tablenumbers 9.49}} \else \AnswerBlank \fi 

\bigskip

\question[5]
Did she accept or reject her hypothesis? Explain why.


\begin{solution}
	
	Reject her hypothesis. Calculated $\chi^2$ is less than the critical value. If calculated is
	less than critical, then she accepts (or fails to reject) the null hypothesis and rejects her research hypothesis.
	
\end{solution}
