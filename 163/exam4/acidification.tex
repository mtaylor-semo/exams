\question[4] \label{q:carbonate_buffering_system}
Write the balanced equilibrium equation for the bicarbonate buffering system in
marine ecosystems, with correct valence (charge) for ions.  %Circle the hydrogen ion 
%that contributes to most increase in acidity.

%\textbf{30 points i based on carbonate buffering system. This seems like
	%	too many points. Replace one of the questions with something about
	%	biological response to acidification.}

\begin{AnswerPage}{0.05\textheight}%
	
	\ce{CO2 + H2O <=> H2CO3 <=> H+ + HCO3- <=> 2H+ + CO3^2-}

\end{AnswerPage}

\question[6]
Explain how the increased acidity (from the equilibrium equation in
question~\ref{q:carbonate_buffering_system}) interacts with \ce{CO3^2-}  dissolved in the ocean water to reduce \ce{CaCO3} availability to marine animals.

\AnswerBox{0.05\textheight}{%
	The hydrogen ion from the bicarbonate buffering system combines with the carbonate ion from the calcium carbonate system to form bicarbonate. (3 pts) This prevents the carbonate ion from being available to organisms that produce calcium carbonate skeletons. (3 pts)
}
