\question[15]
Fruit flies use an enzyme, alcohol dehydrogenase (\emph{adh}), to help metabolize rotting fruit. \emph{adh} has two alleles, fast (F) and slow (S).  A genetic sample of 1553 fruit flies found the following number of individual with each genotype is provided in the first table.

\vspace{\baselineskip}

\begin{minipage}{0.6\linewidth}
Use the information provided in the table at the right to fill in the two tables below with the following information: actual allele frequencies, observed genotype frequencies, and expected genotype frequencies.  Do not calculate the expected number of individuals and do not perform a $\chi^2$ test. The population is not in Hardy-Weinberg Equilibrium.
\end{minipage}\hfill
\begin{minipage}{0.3\linewidth}
	\begin{tabular}{cr}
	\toprule
	Genotype	&	\emph{N}\\
	\midrule
	FF	&	498\\
	FS	&	787\\
	SS	&	268\\
	\bottomrule
	\end{tabular}\vfill
\end{minipage}

\vspace{\baselineskip}
