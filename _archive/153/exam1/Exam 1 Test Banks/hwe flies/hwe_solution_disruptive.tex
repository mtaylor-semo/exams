\question[5]
The fruit fly population (of the previous problem) is not in Hardy-Weinberg equilibrium because the sample contains an excess of both homozygotes and fewer heterozygotes than expected. Assuming natural selection, which mode of selection would fit the observed pattern?  Explain why. 

\begin{solution}
Disruptive selection (2 points). 

Have more of each homozygote and fewer heterozygotes, suggesting both extreme phenotypes are being selected for. (3 points).

\end{solution}
