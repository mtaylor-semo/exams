\question[10]
In the absence of natural selection, two processes are \emph{most likely} to cause the loss of heterozygosity in a single small and isolated population. Name each process.  Then, explain why each is more likely to cause the loss of heterozygosity in small populations compared to large populations.


\begin{solution}
Genetic drift (2 points). Genetic drift is most pronounced in small populations (3 points). \\
Non-random mating (2 pts).  Individuals are more likely to mate with close relatives in small populations (3 points).
\end{solution}
