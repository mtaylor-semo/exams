\question[5]
The fruit fly population (from the previous page) is not in Hardy-Weinberg equilibrium because the sample contains an excess of heterozygotes and fewer homozygotes than expected.  You suspect natural selection might explain the differences between observed and expected frequencies.  Which mode of selection would explain the heterozygote excess?  Explain why. 

\begin{solution}
Stabilizing selection (2 points). 

Have more heterozygotes and fewer of both homozygotes, suggesting both extreme phenotypes are being selected against / heterozygotes are being selected for. (3 points).

\end{solution}
