\question[5]
In the absence of natural selection, which two processes are \emph{most likely} to be important causes of microevolutionary change in a single small and isolated population. (Think about the assumptions of Hardy-Weinberg) Pick one process and explain why or how it it is important cause of evolutionary change in small populations. For 3 points extra credit, name and explain the other process.


\begin{solution}
Genetic drift (2 points). Genetic drift is most pronounced in small populations (3 points). \\
Non-random mating (2 pts).  Individuals are more likely to mate with close relatives in small populations (3 points).
\end{solution}
