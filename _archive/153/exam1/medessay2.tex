\question[10]
In the absence of natural selection, which two microevolutionary processes are \emph{most likely} to occur in single small and isolated population. Justify your choices.



%% Add 'that is less likely or less strong in a large population.' Should I add this or leave as is?

\begin{solution}
Genetic drift (2 points) and Non-random mating (2 pts).  Genetic drift is most pronounced in small populations (3 points). Individuals are more likely to mate with close relatives in small populations (3 points).
\end{solution}
