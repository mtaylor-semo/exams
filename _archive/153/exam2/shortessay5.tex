\question[5]
Assume that you performed an \textsc{anova}. The final probability was $p = 0.035$. Tell me what is the alternative hypothesis that is being tested by an \textsc{anova} and whether the hypothesis would be accepted or rejected, based on your final $p$ value.

\begin{solution}

The alternative hypothesis being tested is that the samples are from different statistical populations. Because $p<0.05$, the alternative hypothesis would be accepted.

\end{solution}
