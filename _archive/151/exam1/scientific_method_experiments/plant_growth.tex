\question[5]
List below the four missing steps of the Scientific Method. Then,
for each step, write a statement that illustrates how scientsts applied
the scientific method to the experiments described below. I have
provided step four as an example.

\textbf{For centuries, people noticed that plants tend to grow or lean
towards a window but, prior to the 18th century, no one knew why plants
responded this way. During the late 1600s to early 1700s, scientists
considered several possible explanations: plants responded to fresh air,
to warmer temperatures, or to light. During those years, several
scientists performed experiments in growth chambers that controlled for
air, temperature, and light. The results of those studies showed that
plants leaned towards light entering the growth chamber from the side
but, in the absence of light, they did not lean towards source of fresh
air or warm tempetures on the sides of the growth chambers. }

%The Five Steps of the Scientific Method:

\begin{parts}
	\part	\vspace{\stretch{0.25}} 
	\ifprintanswers
		\textbf{Observation:} Plants leaned towards the window.
	\fi
	
	\part \vspace{\stretch{1}}
	\ifprintanswers
		\textbf{Hypothesis:} Plants are responding to fresh air from the window.
	\fi
	
	\part \vspace{\stretch{1}}
	\ifprintanswers
		\textbf{Prediction:} If the window is open, then plants will lean toward the window.
	\fi

	\part \vspace{\stretch{1}} \textbf{Experiment and results}: Scientists noted that plants leaned
towards a light source but not to a fresh air source or a warm temperature source.	

	\part \vspace*{\stretch{1}}
	\ifprintanswers
		\textbf{Conclusion:} The hypothesis was falsifiied.
	\fi
\end{parts}
