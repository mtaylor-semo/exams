\question[5]
Describe the difference between the target effect and the rescue effect with respect to predicted and observed turnover rates.

\begin{solution}
	Both are based on higher than expected immigration. Rescue effect occurs on ``near'' islands because high immigration reduces extinction rate (rescuing taxa), lowering the overall turnover rate. Target effect occurs on large islands because higher immigration (large target) replaces extinct taxa more often than expected, creating higher turnover rate.
\end{solution}
