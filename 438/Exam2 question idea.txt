Conservation Bio question ideas

Abbot's Duiker (Cephalophus spadix) is a small deer that is endemic to the Eastern Arc of Tanzania.  The arc is highly fragmented landscape.  Distribution maps are available.  The duiker is endangered.  Have student describe how they might apply the principles of island biogeography to increase survivability of the duiker.  Also include corridor and some of the points raised in the Saunders et al. paper regarding potential downsides to corridors (disease spread, etc.).
